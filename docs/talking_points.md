# Interview talking points (90 seconds)

1. **Why:** Food security, displacement, and child survival are often viewed apart. This dashboard joins them so IM teams see compounded need.
2. **Scope:** Eight Horn/Sahel countries, three public sources, one decision question. Features were cut on purpose.
3. **Displacement definition:** Hosted population (refugees + asylum-seekers + others of concern). Origin and asylum counts are not mixed.
4. **Finding:** Sudan, Ethiopia, and Chad sit in the dual-pressure set. Somalia shows high Phase 3+ with lower hosted displacement. South Sudan’s missing IPC row is a coverage gap, not zero need.
5. **Monday message:** Start joint food-security + protection scrutiny with the dual-pressure countries before single-sector debates.
6. **Limits:** National aggregates; not operational; methods and limitations documented.
